I was test running the boat motor and it burst into flames! New first time experience for me. I put it out with the hose.

The lower carburer's needle was not seating and causing it to cough fuel out the intake. My guess is that the spark came from alternator leads that I left carelessly dangling.

Amazingly, I ran it again later the same day.
