## Original 2006 Post

Current mood: <img src="http://x.myspacecdn.com/images/blog/moods/iBrads/chipper.gif" /> excited
Some of you may have noticed that not too long ago Julie posted a blog in my honor.  I figured since she did that, and since she is totally AWSOME, I would return the favor!  So here goes.

Julie Julie is a coolie
I eat lunch with her at schooly
she isn't mean but she might be a queen
I have never seen her drooly

Julie Julie she is fun
She's on Cross-Country 'cause she likes to run
She's not a giraffe but she makes me laugh
In her myspace picture she's holding a gun

Julie Julie she is super
She is never a party pooper
She's not lame and won the chess4 game
She's an awsome hula hooper

Wow!! that was an excelent piece of work if I do say so myself!  I think it captures what I was trying to say.

I met Julie during cross country.  She was one of many incoming freshman, and I accidentally called her the wrong name once.  But since I did that, I felt really bad, and made sure not to do it again, so she was the first name that I learned!! What an honor!  So anyway, we didn't hang out a whole lot during the season, until toward the end.  Most notably was the time, when she was supposed to come over, and Steve was going to give her a ride, but her parents said she couldn't go, and she went anyway!.  That was totally awsome.  We call it her liberation.  Due to forces outside of our controll Julie never made it to my house that night, but thats not important.  There was also the time when she totally skipped band to hang out with the team after state! that too was awsome.  Mr Ryan smith (aka Kyaker 76) was not too happy about that, and it had some negative effects on her grade, but Come on that was probably among the best few hours of my life.  I mean it was November and some of us (including COLLEEN) jumped in the lake!  And we ate junk food at coach Scott's house.  Of course Julie would know all about junk food as she once ate a VERY VERY old oreo off of my basement floor.  And there was that time when Julie guided me, Kassie, and herself on a tour through Agrabah on Aladdin's magic carpet. That was pretty sweet.  The list could go on and on.

So there you have it.  One of the coolest people in Huron (or maybe even the world for that matter) in a peom and a paragraph.  If you don't know Julie be sure to check out her totally ROCKIHN' myspace!  (She is #1 on my Top 8)  That's all for now.

love, Joshy Woshy
