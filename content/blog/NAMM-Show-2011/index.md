The other guitarist in my band, Dan, managed to get us passes to the 2011 NAMM show, and for free no less.  There was a ton of equipment being demoed, and a good selection of performances.  These pictures do a good job capturing the atmosphere of the show, but they depict way less than one percent of the total content.  This show is huge.
