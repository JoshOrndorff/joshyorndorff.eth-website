In keeping with <a href="thanksgiving-2011">last year</a>'s and 2009's tradition, I invited a guest for Thanksgiving.  And this time it wasn't a guy! Over the summer I met a girl named Maggie while I was teaching at CTY, and kept in contact with her after summer ended.  She came to visit first for Labor Day, and we recently started officially dating.  I don't get to see her often because she lives in Massachusetts, but this Thanksgiving worked out perfectly for a visit.  Enjoy the pictures.
