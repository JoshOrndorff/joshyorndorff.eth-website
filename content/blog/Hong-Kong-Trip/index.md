This album is a selection of pictures from my weekend trip to Hong Kong.  We took a bullet train into Shenzhen, China, which is the city in mainland China that is directly adjacent to Hong Kong.  We stayed there during the nights, and went to Hong Kong during the days.  The city is sweet.  It is a really interesting blend of western and eastern culture.  The crazy part is that almost nobody speaks "Chinese" (ie mandarin) there, so any chinese that we picked up was totally useless.  There are some pretty cool pics I think.  Hopefully this album isn't too big for you to get through, but I thought the city was really cool.
