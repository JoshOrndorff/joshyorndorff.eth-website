My friend Tim was invited to go with his school's principal and her family to see a temple in the far south part of town on Tomb Sweeping Day.  He invited me to come along, and we got up early on our vacation day to meet the family.  Jasmine, spoke great English and served as our tour guide.  Her parents did not speak English, but with body language and a little translation, we could communicate just fine.  The temple is filled with superstitious tradition that will supposedly bring good luck, and Jasmine didn't hesitate to shell out a bit of cash to ensure that she would indeed have good luck.  Tim and I did.  The place was gorgeous and was right on the sea.
