After my <a href="/asheville">Asheville trip</a> and the <a href="/warrior-dash-2012">warrior dash</a>, I started the longest leg of my summer travel by taking a mega bus to Chicago where I met up with George and Kristin Visser and Anne Fennema (again!). We drove to St Louis and picked up Sarah Dugan and then headed on to Gallup, New Mexico for David Dornbos III and Jesi Hale's wedding.  The pictures will tell the story.

After the wedding, I headed on to LA for about ten days, but I couldn't find my camera while I was there so I didn't manage to get any pictures of that part.
