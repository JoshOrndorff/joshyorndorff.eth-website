Sarah got two free nights at Caesar's Palace in Windsor, Ontario between Christmas and new years.  She invited me, her boyfriend John, and her friend Brittany to join her.  We picked up Brittany in Detroit, and headed for the border.  For some reason, the hosers at the crossing thought we were a pretty suspicious-looking crew, so they stopped us and searched our car.  We finally made it out of the penalty box, and got to the hotel.  It was a fun few days of gambling and hanging out, and then we headed to Niagara Falls.
