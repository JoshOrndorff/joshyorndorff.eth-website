It's time for my 2015 New Years resolutions. (See also: <a href="tags/resolutions">Other Years</a>). To be honest, I did terribly with my resolutions last year, not making a single one of them. Some of them will be recycled this year. In fact most of this years resolutions are repeats from previous years that I liked and worked well. Last year was not a good year for me in terms of doing things I like or growing as a person, so this year I'm quitting my job and trying to get back to being the kind of person I can be proud of.


<ul>
<li><h3>Stretch every day</h3>
I'll spend at least 10 minutes stretching each day. I haven't seriously stretched on a regular basis since 2010, and since I'm starting to get older and stiffer and less flexible, I think this will really help.

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> Going Great.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> Went Great.
</li>


<li><h3>Do situps and pushups</h3>
At least 49 pushups and 147 situps each day.

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> Going Great.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> Went Great.
</li>

<li><h3>Get up with my alarm</h3>
I'm terrible about snoozing my alarm. And it makes me rush nearly every morning. I learned recently that my dad gets up with the alarm every time, so I'm going to give it a try. Luckily I'll be unemployed so I probably won't have to set the alarm very much. But these next two weeks (when I'm finishing up my job) might be tough.

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> Going well. A few days I definitely didn't do it, but I'm certainly not doing the daily multi-snooze anymore.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> This went okay. I definitely broke the habit of snoozing every day, but the last few months there were more days that I set an alarm in hopes of getting up on a Saturday and just didn't, or woke up with my alarm on a work day and instead of snoozing, just laid awake for a while instead of getting up.
</li>

<li><h3>No TV at night</h3>
I did this as a monthly resolution back in 2012, but, like most of my resolutions, it didn't stick after the month was over. So this year I'm doing it for a whole year.

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> Going fine. This one has actually been subject to some gray area judgment calls, but I really think I've made the conservative calls.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> I consider this a big success. Occasionally I would fall asleep watching TV in the evening, but then still get up for dinner and situps and pushups, and then not watch TV when I went to bed for the night.
</li>

<li><h3>Take a photo every day</h3>
This is another monthly resolution from 2012. It was a cool idea, but there isn't much change over just one month. So I'll do it for a whole year this time.
<strong>Update 14 October (I think):</strong> Going great. I have a vague memory of accidentally missing one night early on, but otherwise fine.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> This went well. There were two or three more days that I forgot, but I never decided not to do it.
</li>

<li><h3>Build a boat</h3>
This is a repeat from last year. Although I did make some progress learning the process and building models, I really didn't do much on this resolution, so I'm planning to finish it this year. My plan for now is to build a stitch and glue row boat.

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> <a href="/node/1857">Done</a>.
</li>

<li><h3>Biking and running</h3>
Since last year's resolution to "get and stay truly fit" was a massive failure and I'm in the worst shape of my life, I'm going to be more specific. I've learned before that it is important to be specific about resolutions, but I guess some lessons I have to learn over and over. This year my biking and running minimum will be as follows: (running) + (biking / 4) > 2000 km

<strong>Update 14 October:</strong> This is almost definitely not going to happen. I'm currently at 1012.55 km, so basically half way there and about 80% through the year. At least I did way better than last year. Progress is important.
<strong>Year-end Update:</strong> As expected, I didn't complete this one, but at least I did lose a bunch of weight since the beginning of the year, and I'm now in shape where I can easily run 5k whereas at the beginning of the year I was too fat to run at all. I've made this exact same resolution again for <a href="/resolutions-2016">2016</a>.
</li>
