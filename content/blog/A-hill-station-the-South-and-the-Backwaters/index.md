After leaving Delhi, we took a series of trains, buses, and cars to see an observatory where Aditya used to work, the very southern tip of India, and the surrounding areas of both. I saw all the railway classes except the super rich-boy nicest one, and got to take one of the wildest bus ides of my life. The city of Kodia Kanal is 2.4km up into the mountains, and the ride back down caused two different people to puke in / out of the bus. In the southern tip town of Kanyakumari I got in my third ocean of the summer. And then finally, we took a boat tour of "Venice of the east".
